Signature Initiatives

Chapter Programs

Programs and initiatives hosted by the Mu Tau Chapter for campus engagement, leadership, mentorship, and community impact.

Stroll Like An Alpha (SLAA)

A signature program featuring multiple teams competing for the championship trophy. Each team was evaluated in unique performances designed to highlight creativity, coordination, and storytelling. In addition to the competitive athomsphere, a portion of Stroll Like An Alpha funds are donated to March of Dimes, Alpha Phi Alpha's national philanthropic partner.

Ms. Old Gold and Black

A scholarship and recognition pageant highlighting the women of UNC Charlotte's talent, poise, and dedication to education. This serves as a way for particants to showcase their skills and have the opportunity to represent at a higher level.

Barbershop Talk

Open conversations centered around mentorship, leadership, brotherhood, growth, and campus issues. This creates a positive environment and a safe space for students to speak their minds about difficult issues.

Adopt-A-Spot

Apart of the University's campus program, Brothers and other students pick up trash, plastics, and other debries on the campus to preserve the space.

Everybody Needs

Supporting community members through outreach, donations, care packages, and resources.

Nourish Up

Supporting food insecurity initiatives through meal support, food drives, and partnerships.

Mighty Miles

The Mu Tau Chapter's run club on campus encouraging personal health and wellbeing for the students of campus.

MainTaining The Torch

The 2026-2027 year long initative focusing on Brother's Keeper, Alpha Phi Alpha's National Program, where Brothers will being serving the elderly and people struggling in the community. This initative will be reflected within all our programs for the year.

Phinancial Phreedom

Promoting financial literacy, budgeting, investing, and economic empowerment.

Mighty Meals

Feeding and supporting students and community members through meal distributions and outreach. In partnership with the Roof Above as well as our unique chipotle meal handout.

Welcome to the Mu Tau Chapter

The Mu Tau Chapter, the 459th House of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc., continues a strong legacy of leadership, scholarship, service, and brotherhood at UNC Charlotte. Since its chartering in 1977, the chapter has remained committed to uplifting the campus and surrounding community through impactful programming, mentorship, service initiatives, academic excellence, and unforgettable displays of brotherhood through probates, strolls, and chapter traditions. Mu Tau continues marching toward the light while preserving the history and standard established by the men who built the house.

Aims

Manly Deeds, Scholarship, and Love for All Mankind

These aims guide the Mu Tau Chapter’s commitment to leadership, academic excellence, service, brotherhood, and community impact.

Manly Deeds

Upholding character, leadership, discipline, responsibility, and honorable action in every environment we represent.

Scholarship

Promoting academic excellence, intellectual development, achievement, and educational leadership across campus.

Love for All Mankind

Serving the campus and surrounding community through mentorship, outreach, compassion, and brotherhood.
